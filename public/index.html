About the Author

Susan Kaying Pha

Susan Kaying Pha is an author, cultural advocate, and independent publisher dedicated to preserving Hmong history, uplifting voices, and creating meaningful books for all generations.

Through Pha Publishing, she shares stories that celebrate heritage, family, identity, and success — so future generations can see themselves and dream bigger.
