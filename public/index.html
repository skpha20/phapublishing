About the Author

Susan Kaying Pha

Susan Kaying Pha grew up believing that books could change a life. They changed hers.

Today, she is an author, storyteller, and story keeper whose work explores the things that shape us most deeply—family, identity, culture, belonging, courage, and the stories we carry from one generation to the next.

Beyond the page, Susan is a Minnesota State Senator, entrepreneur, community leader, mentor, and advocate for women and girls. For decades, she has dedicated her life to creating opportunities, strengthening communities, and helping others recognize the power of their own voice, story, and potential.

That same purpose lives at the heart of her writing. Whether she is writing about the love between a mother and daughter, the meaning carried within a name, the importance of seeing ourselves reflected in success, or the history and strength of a people, Susan writes to remind readers of who they are, where they come from, and what is possible.

She is the author of My Daughter: From My Heart, For You, Hmong Names, Success That Looks Like Me, and WE ARE HMONG: Fierce and Strong.

At the heart of all her work is one enduring belief: our stories matter and when we share them, they have the power to connect generations, strengthen identity, and change lives.
