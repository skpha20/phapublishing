Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Pha Publishing runs a small website. We are not in the business of collecting information about the people who visit it, and this page explains plainly what does and does not happen when you do.

The short version

This site has no accounts, no logins, and no tracking or advertising software. It sets no cookies of its own. The only information that reaches us automatically is the ordinary server record described below. If a section of this policy seems to promise less data collection than you expect from a website, that is because there genuinely is less.

Information collected automatically

Like nearly every website, the server that delivers these pages keeps a short technical record of each request. That record can include:

  • your IP address;
  • the page or file requested, and the date and time;
  • your browser and operating system, as reported by your browser;
  • the referring page, if you arrived by following a link.

These records exist so the site can be kept running and secure — diagnosing errors, understanding load, and identifying abuse. They are not used to build a profile of you, and they are not sold or shared for marketing.

The newsletter sign-up form

If you submit the form on the home page, your email address is sent to our server and passed to Resend, the service that manages our mailing list, where it is stored so that we can write to you about new books, events, and news from the press. The form asks for nothing else — no name, no other details.

We use your address for that purpose only. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it for anyone else's marketing. Every email we send includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can ask us to remove you at any time by writing to the address at the bottom of this page.

Resend stores and processes the address on our behalf, under its own privacy terms. If sign-ups have not yet been switched on, the form will say so on screen and nothing is stored.

Fonts loaded from Google

The typefaces used on this site are served by Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests those font files directly from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. As with any request your browser makes to another company's server, Google receives your IP address and basic request information in order to deliver the files.

We mention this because it is the one point at which a third party sees anything about your visit, and we would rather say so than leave it out. Google's handling of that data is governed by its own privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser's local storage. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Analytics

We do not use Google Analytics or any comparable analytics or tracking service on this site. Should that change, this policy will be updated first.

Service providers

A small number of companies are involved in running this site:

  • Railway hosts the site and operates the servers that respond to your requests, including the technical records described above.
  • Cloudflare provides the domain name service that points phapublishing.com at those servers, and forwards mail sent to our address.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, as described above.
  • Resend stores our mailing list and delivers the emails we send, if you choose to sign up.

Each handles data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Links to other sites

This site links to other places — booksellers, social media profiles, and similar. Once you follow such a link you are on someone else's website, governed by their privacy practices, not ours. We have no control over what they collect.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

Because we do not maintain accounts or a marketing database through this site, there is generally no stored personal information of yours for us to correct or delete. If you have contacted us directly and would like us to delete that correspondence, or if you have a question about your information, write to us using the address below and we will do our best to help.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information a business holds about you. We will honour such requests to the extent they apply.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change — most likely when the newsletter is connected — we will revise this page and update the date at the top. Material changes will be described here rather than made quietly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@phapublishing.com.

This page describes our practices in good faith and in plain language. It is not legal advice.
